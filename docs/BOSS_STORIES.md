# Campaign boss stories · v8.0.41

A character story for each of the 15 campaign bosses, extending docs/STORY_CANON.md without changing the campaign route, hero unlocks, Grandma/Ruby identities or the ending.

Presentation: first encounter only, one continuous theatrical scene using the existing boss art. Ruby gives context; the boss answers. Fight/Skip continues into the existing reveal. Seen state is saved only on explicit continuation. Replays skip the introduction. Defeat unlocks Read story in Bestiary, including aftermath, without requiring Tier3. Special modes and minibosses retain their existing flow.

Boss dialogue during battle and the final line use these profiles. Existing epilogues still explain the route onward. The C3 generic intro now uses its own story goal instead of the unrelated Frost Prison text.

No new media assets; existing sprite motion respects reduced motion. Canon/text review and phone readability remain owner review. Automated contracts are not a gameplay or visual playtest.

## C1-1 · A Mother’s Bargain

Ruby remembers: the Empress promised the Crown her nest’s freedom so her eggs would survive. Now the crystal orders send even her youngest workers into battle.

**Why fight:** Break the crystal’s hold without abandoning the nest.

**Before:** “If I stop obeying, who will keep my children safe?”

**During:** No! The crystal promised they would be safe! / Workers, stay behind me!

**After:** “The orders are quiet… please, keep the eggs warm.”

## C1-2 · The Keeper of Lost Things

The old drains carried away scraps and forgotten flavors. Clogmaw gathered them so nothing would be lost. The Crown made him hoard until the pipes could no longer breathe.

**Why fight:** Release the memories trapped behind his valves.

**Before:** “You call it waste. I can still hear their names.”

**During:** I will not let another memory wash away! / The pipes are full. There is nowhere left to keep them!

**After:** “The water is clear… I can hear each voice again.”

## C1-3 · One Last Warm Meal

Mr. Griddle once kept the royal kitchen warm. The Crown ordered him to burn stolen flavor as fuel. He has forgotten every recipe except the command to keep the fire alive.

**Why fight:** Stop the engine that turns memories into fuel.

**Before:** “The stove must stay lit. Someone is still waiting for dinner.”

**During:** Just one more tray. Then everyone can eat. / Why does every meal smell like smoke?

**After:** “At last… I can let the stove cool.”

## C1-4 · The Warden Who Would Not Thaw

The Golem was told that frozen prisoners were safe from the Hunger. He guards that promise even as their names fade. Grandma’s own flavor remains inside the Crown, beyond his ice.

**Why fight:** Open the prison; Grandma’s cure still lies ahead.

**Before:** “If the ice melts, what will protect them from forgetting?”

**During:** Stay frozen. Stay safe. That was the order. / Do not take the last shelter I can give them!

**After:** “Their voices… they are warmer than I remember.”

## C1-5 · A Wish With a Mouth

This is only the mouth at the end of a Crown root. It has no childhood to remember: it grew from the wish that no one should hunger, and learned to eat everyone’s flavor instead.

**Why fight:** Cut the feeding root before it drinks the kitchen dry.

**Before:** “A kingdom that cannot remember hunger can never complain.”

**During:** Give me the taste you are trying to save. / You cannot empty a hunger by feeding it!

**After:** “You have cut one root. I have so many more.”

## C2-1 · The Borrowed Daughter

The wounded root grew this Bud from flavors stolen from the garden. She hears the Rootmother calling below and believes that gathering more memories will make her a real, lasting bloom.

**Why fight:** Keep the root from growing a new body.

**Before:** “Mother said I would bloom forever if I brought enough flavor.”

**During:** Do not let my petals fall before she sees me! / Every spore carries a memory. I need them all!

**After:** “Mother… was any of this garden ever mine?”

## C2-2 · One Heart, Too Many Voices

The Behemoth holds the marsh’s memories in one great fungal body. It mistakes every stranger for a wound in its colony. Its poisoned breath is the only way it remembers to protect them.

**Why fight:** Free the people whose memories became the colony.

**Before:** “One heart cannot bear to lose another voice.”

**During:** Stay together. No one leaves the colony! / Breathe with us. Forget the world outside!

**After:** “So many names… they can finally go home.”

## C2-3 · The Queen’s Empty Stores

The hive once shared nectar with the garden. The Crown’s purple ferment spoiled its stores. The Hornet Queen raids the flowers because she cannot admit that her royal honey is starving her own hive.

**Why fight:** Save the nectar before the ferment consumes it.

**Before:** “My workers brought this home. It has to be enough.”

**During:** Every drop belongs in the royal stores! / Do not show my workers an empty flower!

**After:** “The honey… we were feeding them the curse.”

## C2-4 · The Day That Never Ended

When the curse began, the Orchid held the garden’s clock still to save one perfect bloom. Centuries later, all four seasons crowd inside that same day. She fears that moving on means losing everything.

**Why fight:** Let the seasons move forward again.

**Before:** “One more moment. I can still save this day.”

**During:** Spring will mend it. Winter will keep it. Again! / If the hands stop, nothing else has to change!

**After:** “Tomorrow… I had forgotten there could be a tomorrow.”

## C2-5 · The Weight Beneath the Garden

Every stolen flavor in the garden feeds this heart of the Hunger’s new body. The Rootmother calls them her children, yet her roots will not let a single memory return to its owner.

**Why fight:** Break the heart’s grip and follow its oldest root.

**Before:** “I kept every voice alive. Why do they all ask to leave?”

**During:** My roots have held this kingdom for centuries! / Do not ask a mother to loosen her hold!

**After:** “The one who planted me… he is still waiting.”

## C3-1 · The Last Harvest

The Colossus still obeys the harvest oath: leave no field ungathered while anyone is hungry. The Crown has taken every crop. He swings through ash because admitting the harvest is gone would break that oath.

**Why fight:** End the endless harvest and give the soil a chance.

**Before:** “There must be one more row. Someone is still hungry.”

**During:** The granaries cannot be empty. I harvested everything! / Stand back. I have not finished the last row!

**After:** “The fields were green once… plant something for them.”

## C3-2 · A Flower That Forgot Sweetness

The Hollow Bloom was the orchard’s last flower. When the Crown took her flavor, she tried filling the emptiness with everyone else’s. Every stolen taste only made the hollow place larger.

**Why fight:** Return the orchard’s flavors instead of feeding the hollow.

**Before:** “If I take just one more taste, perhaps I will feel it.”

**During:** I remember sweetness. Why can I not taste yours? / Do not leave me empty again!

**After:** “So quiet… I can finally hear the orchard breathe.”

## C3-3 · The Future Behind Glass

The Glass Gardener hid the old kingdom in living panes, hoping one reflection would escape the curse. Now he prunes away every future that might hurt it, including the path that could set it free.

**Why fight:** Show him a future that can survive outside the glass.

**Before:** “Outside, everything breaks. Here, I can still keep them whole.”

**During:** That reflection is the kingdom before we failed it! / I will not let another future shatter!

**After:** “Let them grow in the sun… even if the glass cannot follow.”

## C3-4 · Seeds That Never Touched Soil

The Seedwarden has counted the Crown seeds for centuries. Planting another might repeat the curse; planting none means the kingdom can never recover. He locks away hope because it feels safer than choosing.

**Why fight:** Show him why a living seed matters more than a perfect store.

**Before:** “I know that seed. One has been missing all this time.”

**During:** A closed vault cannot make another mistake! / If one seed changed everything, what might another do?

**After:** “Then take them… plant them well. Do not keep them as I did.”

## C3-5 · The Man Who Feared an Empty Table

The First Planter crowned the First Tree to end a famine. It kept him alive while the kingdom forgot itself. He calls it salvation because the truth means facing centuries of harm.

**Why fight:** Convince him to remove the Crown, not make another wish.

**Before:** “I wanted no one to go hungry. Tell me that was not wrong.”

**During:** I did it to feed the world! Why did their faces disappear? / If I take it off, who will keep them safe from hunger?

**After:** “Every flavor… every person. I remember why I planted it now.”
