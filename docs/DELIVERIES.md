# Latest delivery: v8.3.38 — Fix: heroes became nearly immune from stacked armor; Sesame in QA bots
Owner bug ("at some point the hero stands still and never dies · yellow hexagon ring"). The ring is the Forge Barrier status (`st_p_barrier`), already capped in v8.2.77/78 (15–30% HP, ≤2%/s, pauses after hits). The real cause was armor: Hard Shell (forge mod 14) added armor with no cap while Vital Dough HP is capped at +150; since v8.3.33 every upgrade level scales mods (+4%/level, ×2.6 at Divine +40) and rows may repeat. Measured with `hurtPlayer(100)`: Epic +20 with one Hard Shell per slot took 32, Legend 18, Divine 6.4 (armor 1,464). Fix: `FORGE_ARMOR_CAP=100` (same pattern as `FORGE_HP_CAP`, reset in `applyMeta`) and `DMG_TAKEN_FLOOR=.3` in `hurtPlayer` — armor, talents, gear, guards and low-HP guard together remove at most 70% before Forge DR (cap 30%) and the barrier. After: 100 damage → 33–36 at every tier. QA bots gain two Sesame runs (`build:sesame.runes@6/@11`, casts every recipe in turn; 0 errors). Test: `armor-cap-8338`.
