# Latest delivery: v8.2.57 — Clearer mission VFX for Breach, Hunger Herald and Defend Nectar

Breach floor tint and jagged vein tips now expand with the existing playable radius and retract on the original close timer. The effect is drawn above terrain and under foes. Hunger Herald marks the empowerment target before its beam lands, with directional charge sparks, compact markers beneath empowered monsters, and gate charge highlights. Defend Nectar distinguishes safe, contested, and healing states with a petal base, an exact 135-unit recovery cue, a green-to-red HP bar, and a short bloom after relocation. The new cues reuse stage-owned graphics and keep gameplay timers, stats, damage, rewards and spawn rules intact. Focused graphics tests cover shape, collapse, beam and recovery states, cleanup and reduced motion.

# Previous delivery: v8.2.56 — Frozen Pantry monster army

Frozen Pantry has seven original ice-mochi designs with aligned four-frame stride loops: Infantry, Scout, Lancer, Snowball Thrower, Shield Guard, Captain, and Frost General. Frozen and painted statues keep the same silhouette when they wake, with a cool tint for frozen statues and restored colors for painted ones. Scouts fill one existing basic slot per complete squad on the first visit. Full waves retain the lancer spot. The original spawn cap, enemy classes, officer abilities, damage, mission timers, and rewards are retained. Seven 128px-frame WebP atlases load with the Frozen Pantry mission, including roaming encounters. The prior statues and mob sprites are kept as fallbacks. The Frozen Pantry asset contract covers stage isolation, statue pose, awakening, and the missing-art fallback.
