# Latest delivery: v8.2.67 — Smoother mission props and effects

Phase 2C converts ten active mission loops from four frames to eight. Hunt's corked poison bottle has eight painted rotation poses and now follows the registered animation frame range. The Wisp tether and Hunger Herald beam carry subtle moving highlights. Hunger Splinter, Grandma’s pot, Mirror Path crystal, Last Light lantern, the Trial altar and seed, and the Golem oven keep their original footprints and add subtle light or steam motion. Every loop keeps its original cycle duration and mission loading, with atlas sizes at or below 1024 px per side. The prior art-order donors remain available. The new atlas and loading contract completes phase 2 in the animation roadmap.

# Previous delivery: v8.2.66 — Smoother mission creatures

Phase 2B expands nine mission monster movement loops from four to eight painted frames: Hunger Herald Healer, Booster and Possessor; Seed Rift Fast, Armored and Split carriers; Orchard Bee, Lantern Moth and Hunger Siege Sapper. The atlas frame size, walk or flap cycle duration, lazy stage groups, mission hooks and fallback sprites stay intact. Nine transparent PNGs, art prompts, QA and a preview live in assets/incoming/mission-motion2b. The dedicated contract checks source hashes, frame counts, original cycle duration and the mission load routes. Orders 85 and 87 source records are retained and their integration checks verify the new runtime paths.
