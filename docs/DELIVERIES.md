# Latest delivery: v8.2.58 — Painted Seed Rift and Mirror Path cues

Seed Rift plays a 12-frame (24 FPS) amber-seed intake when a carrier feeds the portal, and Mirror Path plays an 8-frame (24 FPS) prism lock when each mirror charges. Both are original painted one-shots, stage-lazy and scoped to their mission home and roaming load routes, with the old poof or hit ring as fallbacks. Source PNGs, ImageGen prompts, QA and a preview are in mission-vfx-next. The source audit groups 274 registered or physically four-cell sheets and 54 named four-frame ranges as candidates, fixed cells, short bursts, or fallback art. The next production group is Frozen Pantry and mission monsters.

# Previous delivery: v8.2.57 — Clearer mission VFX for Breach, Hunger Herald and Defend Nectar

Breach floor tint and jagged vein tips now expand with the existing playable radius and retract on the original close timer. The effect is drawn above terrain and under foes. Hunger Herald marks the empowerment target before its beam lands, with directional charge sparks, compact markers beneath empowered monsters, and gate charge highlights. Defend Nectar distinguishes safe, contested, and healing states with a petal base, an exact 135-unit recovery cue, a green-to-red HP bar, and a short bloom after relocation. The new cues reuse stage-owned graphics and keep gameplay timers, stats, damage, rewards and spawn rules intact. Focused graphics tests cover shape, collapse, beam and recovery states, cleanup and reduced motion.
