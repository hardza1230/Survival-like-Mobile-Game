# Latest delivery: v8.3.32 — Story bosses keep their move gaps; smaller Mint Glacier Bloom

# Previous delivery: v8.3.31 — Boss Rage off, single loading screen, no stage ambience, New Discovery fix
Owner 27 Nov. (4) Bug: after C1-2 the New Discovery popup popped and closed repeatedly — art loads rebuilt the Lobby on top of the open reveal; `buildMenuScreen` now waits while a reveal is on screen. Tests: `owner-8331`, `audio-a23-living-battlefield`.
