# Latest delivery: v8.2.56 — Frozen Pantry monster army

Frozen Pantry has seven original ice-mochi designs with aligned four-frame stride loops: Infantry, Scout, Lancer, Snowball Thrower, Shield Guard, Captain, and Frost General. Frozen and painted statues keep the same silhouette when they wake, with a cool tint for frozen statues and restored colors for painted ones. Scouts fill one existing basic slot per complete squad on the first visit. Full waves retain the lancer spot. The original spawn cap, enemy classes, officer abilities, damage, mission timers, and rewards are retained. Seven 128px-frame WebP atlases load with the Frozen Pantry mission, including roaming encounters. The prior statues and mob sprites are kept as fallbacks. The Frozen Pantry asset contract covers stage isolation, statue pose, awakening, and the missing-art fallback.

# Previous delivery: v8.2.55 — Mission card polish and clearer battle effects

Orders 92 and 93 are integrated, with three additional painted sheets for Hunger Crack and Momo Shotgun. Mission cards share the Heart Seed back, foil sweep and flip flash. Hunt retains its actual danger footprint under the painted timer. Wisp swaps the escort area, tether and outside-zone shadows as the player crosses the boundary. Hunger Crack has separate fissure states and capped spawn bursts. Shotgun distinguishes the regular shot, Double Barrel opening shot and actual follow-up. The added runtime files total 539,250 bytes (9.59 MiB decoded across all groups) and load with the stage or Momo run. Existing damage, hit areas, wave and shot timing are preserved.
