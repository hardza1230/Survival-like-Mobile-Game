# Character unlocks and future releases (owner decision · 27 Sep 2026)

## Core roster: permanent story rewards

The five original fighters are never sold for Sugar or real money. A first clear of the named stage grants the fighter permanently; replaying a stage, Ascension, and league changes must not remove ownership. Previously purchased fighters remain owned. New saves always start with Strawberry.

| Fighter | Story milestone | Current release state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strawberry (`momo`) | Starting fighter | Available |
| Mint (`mint`) | First clear of Chapter 1 Stage 1 (index 0) | Available; stage grant implemented in v5.76 |
| Cocoa (`cocoa`) | First clear of Chapter 1 Stage 5 (index 4) | Available; stage grant implemented in v5.76 |
| Taro (`taro`) | Planned: first clear of Chapter 2 Stage 5 (overall Stage 10, index 9) | Character work unfinished. Do not open new acquisition until ready. Existing owners keep access. |
| Sesame (`sesame`) | Planned: first clear of Chapter 3 Stage 5 (overall Stage 15, index 14) | Character work unfinished. Do not open new acquisition until ready. Existing owners keep access. |

When Taro and Sesame are ready, ship their gameplay and milestones together. Backfill the grant from permanent clear history for players who already passed the stage. Ensure Chapter 3 completion grants all five core fighters even when a player has ascended since the first clear. Keep a durable historical completion record separate from resettable Mastery if needed. No player should have to repeat the entire story because a character arrived later.

## Character cards

Cards show a concise play style, signature weapon, stats, current ownership, and stage requirement. An info control opens a readable description. Incomplete fighters say “In development” without a purchase action. New unlocks set a Heroes reminder; opening Heroes clears it.

## Future fighters and league policy

Additional fighters beyond the five core characters may be offered for real money for early access in their launch league. In older leagues they become playable for free. Exact timing, eligibility, account entitlement, pricing, billing integration, refunds, and platform policy are **not implemented or finalized**. Do not silently attach a Sugar price or lock an already owned fighter when leagues change. Before building commerce, specify a league identifier, launch/end dates, entitlement record, free release rule, restore purchase flow, and store review requirements.

This document records the product direction only; no real-money purchase UI or billing is authorized by this document alone.
