# Chapter 1 Continuous Combat

The development branch enables continuous combat on first clears of C1-1 through C1-5. Use `?combat83=0` to compare the legacy mission flow. The tutorial, replay, recipe, rift and boss-rush paths keep their existing flow.

Warm-up, Pressure, Miniboss, Recovery, Escalation, Swarm and Final Boss use the existing stage time and enemy budgets. Ordinary transitions preserve enemies, projectiles and pickups. Story arrivals and boss rewards retain their existing paths. The phase HUD shows the active time and progress.

The Combat Director selects phase-appropriate groups from Ambush, Stampede, Commander, Barrage, Pincer, Hunter Pack, Fortress, Hazard Shift, Loot Rush and Last Stand. It uses each stage's enemy art, respects the remaining live slots and reuses the normal spawn budget. Commander, Fortress and Hazard Shift are introduced later in the chapter.

Two encounters can start automatically, one in Pressure and one after the Miniboss. They are drawn without replacement from short versions of Hunger’s Mouth, Frozen Pantry and Grandma’s Pot. Targets, instructions and a timer appear while combat continues. Completion drops Sugar. Expiration carries no extra penalty. A single event can run at once. The director leaves fewer slots for ordinary refills during a side event, and the wave boundary clears encounter state before a boss entrance. These are short field versions of the three mechanics; the full League versions and other chapters retain their existing rules.

No playtest or automated suite was run for the Chapter 1 expansion at the owner's request. The earlier accelerated C1-4 check applied to the previous prototype.
