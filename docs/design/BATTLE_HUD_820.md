# Compact ink battle HUD 8.1.20

The owner requested expressive anime ink linework, complete necessary information and a less bulky HUD. This keeps Mochi Mayhem's plum, ivory, mint and fruit accents, existing characters and skill icons. It uses original brushwork with no franchise insignia or imported character art.

Four new transparent ImageGen skins replace the existing top-island, mission, boss and three-state button skins. Original source, actual prompt, source crop records and hashes remain in `assets/incoming/hud_ink820/`; a mechanical packer trims at clear source gutters and halves the art into lossless WebP. Total runtime art is145,280bytes. Existing stage-only keys keep it out of boot loading; text and numbers remain live game text.

The first row retains level, KOs, Sugar, speed, pause and sound. Waves and pips share a small row; the mission sits directly underneath with a four-pixel progress tube. Boss encounters replace the redundant encounter heading with a compact39px name/health strip. The live fourteen-pixel tube includes phase, HP and percentage; wide screens cap the strip at440px. An ongoing mission remains visible alongside a boss. Hunger/replay progress and season icons retain their own data. Player HP/EXP, skills, Unique and Dash remain in their familiar locations; touch routing, positions, sizes and handedness settings are unchanged.

Boss fluid changes continuously from green at full HP through yellow and orange to coral red near empty. Subtle surface lines, three small bubbles, bounded meniscus and a short pale delayed-damage trail add motion. Exponential interpolation has the same drain duration at30/60/120FPS. Actual boss HP drives the target; a new actor/spawn resets the fluid and trail. Hiding the HUD clears the trail. Pause holds the clock. Low effects/reduced motion show accurate static health and a steady readiness outline.

Relics show all five normal slots, with an overflow count for older or exceptional saves; the existing relic detail cards remain in Pause. All active status icons and amounts remain visible and wrap within the viewport; run trait shares the footer row when there is room. Ready outlines and counter feedback are restrained. Hidden Unique controls no longer leave a backdrop behind Sesame's rune controls. None of these changes alters attacks, damage, cooldowns, mission rules or rewards.

Validation covers independent original-PNG alpha decoding and complete exclusive source-cut coverage, exact art hashes, liquid color/flow/trail bounds, FPS parity, owner replacement, hidden controls, pause/Low/reduced and current input-ownership contracts. A real Phaser Canvas review loads production fonts, nine-slices and HUD methods with synthetic UI data only:56 fixtures cover wave, idle, boss, boss+mission, seasons, replay and all eight statuses at four viewport sizes with reduced motion on/off; all handedness/control-size positions are compared with the existing rules. No combat simulation or screenshots to judge appearance. Owner checks final feel on the phone.
