# Affix Mod Catalog

Version: 4.46.0

The Affix Forge has 15 non-duplicate mods. T1 is the strongest tier and T5 is the entry tier. Every equipment slot has at least four compatible results so a Rare item can fill all four lines.

## Offense — orange

| Mod | Slots | T5 → T1 | Gameplay role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Damage | Weapon, Gloves, Amulet, Ring | +3–4% → +13–16% | Reliable damage for every build |
| Crit | Weapon, Gloves, Amulet, Ring | +2% → +6–7% | More critical hits |
| Crit DMG | Weapon, Gloves, Ring | +8–14% → +45–60% | Raises critical-hit payoff |
| Cooldown | Weapon, Gloves, Amulet, Ring | -2% → -6–8% | Faster skill cycles |
| Boss DMG | Weapon, Ring | +4–6% → +17–21% | Multiplies damage to bosses and minibosses |
| Low-HP DMG | Weapon, Gloves, Ring | +3–4% → +11–14% | Risk/reward damage while below 40% HP |

## Roulette reveal

- The outcome is rolled, paid for and saved atomically before the reveal begins.
- A three-card reel cycles through compatible mods, then slows over 12 beats before locking the center card.
- Craft input is disabled during the reveal to prevent double spending.
- The final card inherits its category color and shows the rolled value and tier.
- The pool preview shows up to six cards in portrait mode, category counts and the number of additional possible results.
