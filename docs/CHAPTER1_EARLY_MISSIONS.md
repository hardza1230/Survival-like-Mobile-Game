# Chapter 1: guided Stage 1–3 wave plan (27 Sep 2026)

Wave 2 is the miniboss in all three stages. Waves 1, 3 and 4 use a fixed order in ordinary story runs, including replays and all difficulty levels. Boss Rush, Recipe and Rift modes keep their own flows. A failed objective still advances under the existing wave-clear rules; it never blocks the stage indefinitely.

| Stage | Wave 1 | Wave 2 | Wave 3 | Wave 4 | Lesson |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Sour Ant Nest | Fill the meter → Survive | Miniboss | Survive | Hunt 2 marked elites | Move → survive → follow marked targets |
| 2 Rotting Drain | Survive | Miniboss | Hunt marked elites | Escort the Wisp | Read enemy signals → protect a moving ally |
| 3 Chili Engine Room | Capture the ring | Miniboss | Escort the Wisp | Hunt marked guards | Repeat known verbs under greater pressure |

Each wave banner gives a direct instruction and a “Mission 1/3” style progress label. The HUD still shows objective progress. Stage 4 onward retains its existing objective selection until a separate plan is approved. Playtest on a real phone before adjusting time, target counts, and damage; check that Wisp navigation and marked targets remain readable on a crowded screen.
