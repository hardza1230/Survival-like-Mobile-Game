# Image generation prompts — ant animation commit 2

Built-in imagegen; existing generated ant artwork supplied as identity references. Six full sheets plus two targeted action repairs. Final PNGs are packed transparently with one common scale per species.

## worker

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: production monster sprite sheet for Mochi Mayhem mobile game. Input image is the identity reference; preserve its species, anatomy, colors and signature silhouette. Create ONE transparent sprite sheet, exactly 4 columns by 4 rows of equal square cells, 16 poses of the SAME brown caramel ant with raspberry-purple eyes, six articulated legs, pale sugar crystal backpack. Facing RIGHT in consistent 3/4 top-down view throughout, polished 2.5D candy-fantasy, dark colored outlines, upper-left light. Full sheet desired 1024x1024. Every pose fully inside its own cell with generous 12% margins. Same body size, same head/abdomen center, matching foot ground line at 85% cell height. NO camera zoom or growing/shrinking. NO cast shadows, no ground, no labels/numbers/grid. Read LEFT TO RIGHT then TOP TO BOTTOM: frames 0-5 are six smooth chronological walking tripod-gait poses with clearly alternating LEG POSITIONS and planted feet, not merely body deformation; frames 6-7 calm idle breathing, same position; frames 8-11 four consecutive mandible bite: lower head, open jaws, snap jaws, recover poses; frames 12-13 hurt flinch and recovery with body size stable; frames 14-15 collapse and faded candy-crumble defeat, remaining within same cell. Draw actual changed limbs and joints in every walk pose. No repeated identical walk pictures. No extra monsters. True alpha background everywhere between silhouettes.
